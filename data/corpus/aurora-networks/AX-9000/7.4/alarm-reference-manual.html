AX-9000 Alarm Reference — AuroraOS 7.4

This manual lists every alarm raised by AuroraOS 7.4, its severity and the runbook to use.

AX-ALM-1201 RP CPU above 90%

Severity: Major. Raised when route processor CPU stays above 90% for 60 seconds. Cleared when CPU falls below 75%. Runbook: RB-103.

AX-ALM-2031 Line card temperature critical

Severity: Critical. Raised when a line card inlet sensor reads 85 °C or higher. The card powers off automatically at 95 °C. Runbook: RB-105.

AX-ALM-3007 Interface error threshold crossed

Severity: Minor. Raised when CRC or input errors exceed 100 per minute on any interface. Runbook: RB-102.

AX-ALM-4102 BGP peer down

Severity: Major. Raised when a configured BGP neighbor leaves the Established state. Runbook: RB-101.

AX-ALM-5001 Power supply failure

Severity: Critical. Raised when a PSU reports output failure. The AX-9000 runs on N+1 power; replace the failed PSU within 24 hours. Check with show environment power.
